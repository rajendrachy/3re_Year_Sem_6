# ELEMENTARY MENSURATION – Question Wise Complete Formula Sheet

---

# Q1. Volume of Cuboid

## Formula Used

Volume of Cuboid = Length × Breadth × Height

where:
- Length = longer side
- Breadth = shorter side
- Height = vertical side

---

# Q2. Total Surface Area of Cuboid

## Formula Used

Total Surface Area = 2[(Length × Breadth) + (Breadth × Height) + (Height × Length)]

---

# Q3. Diagonal of Cuboid

## Formula Used

(Diagonal)² = (Length)² + (Breadth)² + (Height)²

Diagonal = √[(Length)² + (Breadth)² + (Height)²]

---

# Q13. Sphere Formula

## Surface Area

Surface Area = 4π × (Radius)²

## Volume

Volume = (4/3) × π × (Radius)³

---

# Q14. Slant Height of Cone

## Radius

Radius = Diameter / 2

## Pythagoras Theorem

(Slant Height)² = (Radius)² + (Height)²

Slant Height = √[(Radius)² + (Height)²]

---

# Q15. Percentage of Wood Wasted

## Volume of Cone

Volume = (1/3) × π × (Radius)² × Height

## Volume of Cuboid

Volume = Length × Breadth × Height

## Percentage Wasted

Percentage Wasted = [(Volume of Cuboid − Volume of Cone) / Volume of Cuboid] × 100

---

# Q16. Total Surface Area of Cone

## Slant Height

(Slant Height)² = (Radius)² + (Height)²

## Total Surface Area

Total Surface Area = π × Radius × (Radius + Slant Height)

---

# Q17. Volume of Frustum

## Circumference Formula

Circumference = 2π × Radius

Radius = Circumference / (2π)

## Volume of Frustum

Volume = (1/3) × π × Height × [(Radius1)² + (Radius2)² + (Radius1 × Radius2)]

# Q20. Smaller Cubes from Big Cube

Volume = (Side)³

# Q21. Weight of Iron Pipe

## Outer Radius

Outer Radius = Inner Radius + Thickness

## Volume of Pipe

Volume = π × Length × [(Outer Radius)² − (Inner Radius)²]

## Weight

Weight = Volume × Weight per cubic cm

# Q22. Sphere Drawn into Wire

Volume of Sphere = Volume of Wire

# Q23. Cylinder Melted into Sphere

## Volume of Cylinder

Volume = π × (Radius)² × Height

Volume of Cylinder = Volume of Sphere

---

# Q24. Cylinder Melted into Spheres

## Volume of Cylinder

Volume = π × (Radius)² × Height

## Volume of Sphere

Volume = (4/3) × π × (Radius)³

## Number of Spheres

Number = Volume of Cylinder / Volume of One Sphere

---

# Q25. Greatest Sphere inside Cone

## Slant Height

(Slant Height)² = (Radius)² + (Height)²

## Radius of Sphere

Radius of Sphere = (Radius of Cone × Height of Cone) / (Radius of Cone + Slant Height)

---

# Q26. Ratio of Volumes of Spheres

## Surface Area of Sphere

Surface Area = 4π × (Radius)²

## Volume of Sphere

Volume = (4/3) × π × (Radius)³

## Ratio Rule

If Surface Area Ratio = a : b

Then Radius Ratio = √a : √b

Then Volume Ratio = (√a)³ : (√b)³

---

# Q27. Curved Surface Area of Cylinder

## Formula

Curved Surface Area = 2π × Radius × Height

## New Radius

New Radius = 3 × Radius

## New Height

New Height = (1/3) × Height

---

# Q28. Cone Formed by Rotating Triangle

## Volume of Cone

Volume = (1/3) × π × (Radius)² × Height

## Slant Height

(Slant Height)² = (Radius)² + (Height)²

## Curved Surface Area

Curved Surface Area = π × Radius × Slant Height

## Total Surface Area

Total Surface Area = π × Radius × (Radius + Slant Height)

---

# Q29. Percentage Increase in Cuboid Volume

## Original Volume

Volume = Length × Breadth × Height

## New Volume

New Volume = (2 × Length) × (3 × Breadth) × (4 × Height)

## Percentage Increase

Percentage Increase = [(New Volume − Original Volume) / Original Volume] × 100

---

# Q30. Length Made Four Times

## Original Volume

Volume = Length × Breadth × Height

## New Volume

New Volume = (4 × Length) × Breadth × Height

## Percentage Increase

Percentage Increase = [(New Volume − Original Volume) / Original Volume] × 100

---

# Q31. Edge of Cube Increased by 10%

## Volume of Cube

Volume = (Side)³

## New Side

New Side = 1.10 × Side

## New Volume

New Volume = (1.10)³ × Original Volume

## Percentage Increase

Percentage Increase = [(New Volume − Original Volume) / Original Volume] × 100
