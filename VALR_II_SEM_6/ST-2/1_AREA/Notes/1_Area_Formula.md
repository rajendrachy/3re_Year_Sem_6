
# Mensuration Formulas

## 2D Shape Area Formulas

### Rectangle
\[
\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{breadth}
\]

### Square
\[
\text{Area} = (\text{side})^2
\]

### Triangle
\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}
\]

### Triangle (Heron's Formula)
\[
\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}
\]

where,

\[
s = \frac{a+b+c}{2}
\]

(semi-perimeter)

### Equilateral Triangle
\[
\text{Area} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2
\]

### Parallelogram
\[
\text{Area} = \text{base} \times \text{height}
\]

### Rhombus
\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times d_1 \times d_2
\]

### Trapezium
\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}(a+b)h
\]

where:

- \(a\) and \(b\) are parallel sides
- \(h\) is height

### Circle
\[
\text{Area} = \pi r^2
\]

(\(r\) = radius)

### Circle Sector
\[
\text{Area} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\pi r^2
\]

(\(\theta\) is the angle in degrees)

### Area of a Ring/Path
\[
\text{Area} = \pi(R^2-r^2)
\]

where:

- \(R\) = Outer radius
- \(r\) = Inner radius

# Important Perimeter Formulas

### Rectangle
\[
\text{Perimeter} = 2(l+b)
\]

### Square
\[
\text{Perimeter} = 4a
\]

### Circle (Circumference)
\[
\text{Circumference} = 2\pi r
\]

### Semi-circle Perimeter
\[
\text{Perimeter} = \pi r + 2r
\]

---

# 3D Shape Surface Area Formulas

## Cube
\[
\text{Surface Area} = 6a^2
\]

## Cuboid
\[
\text{Surface Area} = 2(lb+bh+hl)
\]

## Cylinder

### Curved Surface Area (CSA)
\[
2\pi rh
\]

### Total Surface Area (TSA)
\[
2\pi r(h+r)
\]

## Cone

### Curved Surface Area (CSA)
\[
\pi rl
\]

### Total Surface Area (TSA)
\[
\pi r(l+r)
\]

where:

\[
l = \sqrt{r^2+h^2}
\]

(slant height)

## Sphere
\[
\text{Surface Area} = 4\pi r^2
\]

## Hemisphere

### Curved Surface Area
\[
2\pi r^2
\]

### Total Surface Area
\[
3\pi r^2
\]

# Key Tips for Aptitude

- Always check that units are consistent before calculation.
- Convert cm to m or vice versa when required.

## Diagonal Formulas

### Diagonal of Square
\[
d = a\sqrt{2}
\]

### Diagonal of Rectangle
\[
d = \sqrt{l^2+b^2}
\]

## Area of 4 Walls
\[
\text{Area} = 2h(l+b)
\]

where:

- \(l\) = length
- \(b\) = breadth
- \(h\) = height
