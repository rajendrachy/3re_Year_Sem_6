# Isosceles Right Triangle

An isosceles right triangle is a triangle that has:
- Two equal sides
- One right angle (90°)

It is also called a **45°–45°–90° triangle** because the other two angles are equal.

## Properties

- One angle = 90°
- Other two angles = 45° each
- Two sides are equal

## Hypotenuse Formula

If each equal side is `a`, then:

c = a√2

where:
- `a` = equal sides
- `c` = hypotenuse

## Perimeter Formula

P = 2a + a√2

or

P = a(2 + √2)

where:
- `a` = equal side length

## Area Formula

A = a² / 2

where:
- `A` = area
- `a` = equal side length

## Example

If `a = 8 cm`:

- Hypotenuse = `8√2 cm`
- Perimeter = `16 + 8√2 cm`
- Area = `32 cm²`
